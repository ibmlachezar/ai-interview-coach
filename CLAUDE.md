# AI Interview Coach — Claude Code Setup

Built by [Lachezar Atanasov](https://lachezaratanasov.com)

---

## How to use

1. Paste your job description into `job-description.md`
2. Paste your background into `my-background.md`
3. Run: `read the file .claude/commands/interview-prep.md and follow the instructions`
4. Your prep kit saves to `outputs/interview-prep.md`

---

## What you get

- 10 tailored interview questions across 4 categories
- Ideal answers using your actual background
- Stories to prepare from your experience
- What NOT to say for each question
- 3 red flags to watch for in this process
- One coaching insight specific to you and this role

---

## File structure

```
ai-interview-coach/
├── job-description.md     ← paste the job description here
├── my-background.md       ← paste your resume/experience here
├── outputs/               ← your prep kit saves here
├── .claude/
│   └── commands/
│       └── interview-prep.md  ← the skill
└── CLAUDE.md
```
